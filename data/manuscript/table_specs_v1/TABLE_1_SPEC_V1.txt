TABLE 1 — BENCHMARK AND EXPERT PERFORMANCE
==========================================

PURPOSE
Summarize the frozen benchmark and final TEST performance of the two
independently developed experts and their simple probability average.

BENCHMARK
TRAIN proteins: 38,031
VAL proteins: 4,754
TEST proteins: 4,754
GO-MF labels: 494
Paired structure-eligible TEST proteins: 4,731
Structure-ineligible TEST proteins: 23

PRIMARY PERFORMANCE POPULATION
All reported model-performance metrics in this table use the frozen paired
structure-eligible TEST population (n=4,731) and the same 494 frozen GO-MF
labels.

Macro AUPRC is calculated over the 484 labels with at least one positive
annotation in this paired TEST population; 10 labels have zero TEST positives.

MODELS
1. Sequence expert
   - Frozen ESM-2 representation:
     facebook/esm2_t12_35M_UR50D
   - ESM-2 parameters: 33,500,401
   - ESM-2 frozen, not fine-tuned
   - Trainable MLP head parameters: 500,718
   - Final TEST probabilities use global temperature scaling:
     T = 1.106977105140686

2. Structure expert
   - Geometric residue-graph GNN
   - Trainable parameters: 298,990
   - No ESM-2 input
   - pLDDT is not a model input
   - Final TEST probabilities are uncalibrated:
     T = 1.0; calibration_applied = false

3. Simple probability average
   - Arithmetic mean of sequence and structure probabilities
   - No additional trainable parameters

REPORTING NOTES
- AUPRC means average precision.
- Fmax and Smin are reported from the frozen dedicated Fmax/Smin evaluation.
- Thresholds used to identify Fmax/Smin are evaluation summaries and were not
  used for model selection or final TEST optimization.
- Distinguish the frozen ESM-2 representation-model parameter count from the
  trainable sequence-head parameter count.
- Do not imply that structure temperature scaling improved calibration.
- Do not describe the structure expert as sequence-independent because
  amino-acid identity is part of its node features.
- Do not interpret the simple average as outperforming the sequence expert.
