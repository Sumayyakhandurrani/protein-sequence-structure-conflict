FIGURE 5 SPECIFICATION V1
========================

Figure ID
---------
Figure 5

Working title
-------------
Residue-level explanations show functional-site enrichment,
deletion faithfulness, conflict relevance, and high stability

Primary purpose
---------------
Summarize the frozen E10 explainability analyses.

The figure tests whether residue-level attribution patterns produced
for the sequence and structure experts have measurable relationships
with annotated functional residues, whether attribution-ranked
residues influence model predictions under deletion, whether residues
identified by the conflict-driver analysis produce greater disagreement
reduction than matched random perturbations, and whether explanations
are stable under the frozen stability analysis.

This figure is supporting evidence about the behavior and quality of
the explanation signals.

It MUST NOT be interpreted as demonstrating biological causality.


============================================================
SCIENTIFIC INTERPRETATION BOUNDARY
============================================================

Allowed interpretation
----------------------
The E10 analyses provide evidence that:

1. residue-level explanation scores are enriched at annotated
   functional sites relative to matched random residues;

2. deleting highly attributed residues has substantially greater
   prediction impact than the matched/random deletion reference used
   by the frozen faithfulness analysis;

3. joint perturbation of conflict-driver-ranked residues reduces
   sequence–structure disagreement more than matched random
   perturbations;

4. residue explanations are highly stable under the frozen stability
   evaluation for both modalities.


Prohibited interpretation
-------------------------
DO NOT claim that:

- XAI proves biological causality;
- the attribution method discovers causal residues;
- attributed residues are necessarily mechanistic determinants of
  molecular function;
- the structure expert is sequence-independent;
- one modality is biologically superior because its numerical
  enrichment or faithfulness value is larger;
- differences between sequence and structure values establish
  statistically significant modality superiority unless such a test
  was explicitly performed;
- explanation stability establishes biological correctness.

The figure should use descriptive wording such as:

"functional-site enrichment"
"deletion faithfulness"
"conflict-driver enrichment"
"explanation stability"

Avoid wording such as:

"causal residues"
"biological proof"
"mechanistic validation"
"structure is better than sequence"
