============================================================
STYLE REQUIREMENTS
============================================================

1. Publication-style scientific schematic.

2. Minimal decorative graphics.

3. No protein cartoon implying experimentally determined structure for
   every protein.

4. Clearly separate sequence and structure branches.

5. Avoid implying that structure is independent of amino-acid identity.

6. Avoid causal arrows from conflict to biological failure.
   Arrows indicate computational flow, not biological causality.

7. Use consistent notation:
   p_seq
   p_struct
   U
   JS

8. Keep all labels readable at journal-column scaling.

9. Do not include performance numbers; those begin in Figure 2.

10. Caption must define the post-confirmatory-status distinction.


============================================================
CAPTION CONTENT REQUIREMENTS
============================================================

Caption should state that:

- reviewed proteins with experimental GO-MF annotations were used;
- a leakage-controlled Graph-V2 benchmark was constructed;
- sequence and structure-derived experts were developed independently;
- calibration was independently assessed for both experts; the sequence temperature adjustment was retained, whereas the fitted structure temperature was rejected;
- sequence–structure conflict was quantified per protein-label pair;
- the central analysis asks whether conflict adds reliability
  information beyond expert uncertainty;
- subsequent analyses examine arbitration, homology, perturbation,
  GO-frequency, biological explanation, negative controls, and
  backbone sensitivity;
- designated robustness/XAI analyses were post-confirmatory.
