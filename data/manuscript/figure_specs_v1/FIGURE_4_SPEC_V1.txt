FIGURE_4_SPEC_V1
================

TITLE
-----
Conflict reliability persists across GO-frequency strata and an alternative structure backbone


PURPOSE
-------
Figure 4 tests two complementary robustness questions for the central
structure-expert reliability result:

1. Does the incremental reliability information from sequence-structure
   conflict persist across GO Molecular Function labels with different
   TRAIN-set support?

2. Does the central structure-failure reliability result persist when the
   primary structure GNN is replaced by an alternative GCN-style structure
   backbone?

The figure is a robustness/sensitivity analysis.

It must NOT be interpreted as:
- evidence that increasing GO-term frequency causally increases the usefulness
  of conflict;
- evidence that the alternative GCN is superior to the primary structure GNN;
- evidence of robustness across all GNN architectures;
- evidence of robustness across multiple protein language models;
- evaluation of unseen or extremely rare GO terms below TRAIN support 75.

METRIC
------
Incremental average precision for predicting structure-expert failure:

Delta AP = AP(MB) - AP(MA)

where:

MA:
    U_structure
    U_sequence

MB:
    U_structure
    U_sequence
    Bernoulli_JS conflict

Positive Delta AP means that adding sequence-structure conflict improves
structure-failure reliability discrimination beyond expert uncertainty alone.

CLAIMS ALLOWED
--------------
1. Conflict provides positive incremental structure-failure reliability
   information across all evaluated TRAIN-support bands.

2. The effect is present even in the lowest included support band, 75-99.

3. The positive structure-side reliability effect persists under the
   alternative GCN-style backbone.

4. These analyses strengthen the robustness of the central structure-side
   reliability finding.


CLAIMS NOT ALLOWED
------------------
1. Increasing GO frequency causes conflict to become more useful.

2. Conflict was tested on unseen or extremely rare labels below support 75.

3. Alternative GCN is superior to the primary GNN.

4. The result is robust across all GNN architectures.

5. The result is robust across multiple PLM backbones.

6. Conflict universally improves GO-MF prediction.

7. The backbone comparison is a formal architecture benchmark.
