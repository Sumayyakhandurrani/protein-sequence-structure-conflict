====================================================
FIGURE-WIDE INTERPRETATION
====================================================

Panel A:
Conflict provides substantial incremental information for predicting
structure-expert failure beyond expert uncertainty.

Panel B:
Destroying protein-level correspondence removes the earlier
absolute-disagreement reliability benefit.

Panel C:
Conflict improves arbitration relative to uncertainty-only gating,
but does not beat the stronger sequence expert overall.

Panel D:
Strong opposing predictions form a small, distinct regime in which
sequence is closer more often, while structure remains closer for a
non-trivial minority.
