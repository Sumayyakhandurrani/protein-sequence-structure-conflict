FIGURE 3 — ROBUSTNESS AND MODALITY ASYMMETRY
=============================================

PURPOSE
-------
Test whether the incremental reliability information carried by
sequence–structure conflict persists across low-homology regimes and
under controlled modality degradation, while showing that the effect
is asymmetric between the structure and sequence experts.

All panels use frozen existing outputs.
No models are retrained during figure generation.

====================================================
FIGURE-WIDE INTERPRETATION
====================================================

1. The structure-side conflict reliability signal persists across
   adequately populated low-homology TEST strata.

2. Controlled perturbations measurably degrade the targeted experts.

3. Conflict remains strongly informative for structure-expert failure
   under coordinate degradation.

4. The corresponding incremental AP for sequence-expert failure is
   near zero or negative under sequence masking.

5. Therefore the reliability value of sequence–structure conflict is
   modality-asymmetric rather than universally symmetric.


====================================================
CLAIMS NOT TO MAKE
====================================================

- Do not claim conflict universally improves protein-function prediction.
- Do not claim conflict improves both experts equally.
- Do not claim monotonic conflict increase with perturbation severity.
- Do not claim causal effects of sequence identity.
- Do not describe "no qualifying hit" as absence of homology.
- Do not claim a perfect >=30% cross-split homology guarantee.
- Do not use the n=8 >=30% stratum for strong inference.
- Do not invent E6 confidence intervals.
- Do not imply CAL-EVAL perturbation results are FINAL TEST results.
